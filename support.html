Support

We usually answer within 24 hours.

Email us at abs.app@gmail.com, or in the app open Settings → Contact support.

I got a new iPhone. How do I get my progress back?

Sign into the same iCloud account, install 150 Abs and tap Restore my progress on the welcome screen. Your streak, history, friends, crews and duels come back.

How do I restore Plus?

Settings → Plus → Restore purchases, signed into the Apple ID you used to buy it.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → 150 Abs Plus, or in the app: Settings → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal (or before your free trial ends) to avoid being charged.

I missed a day. Is my streak gone?

A Streak Shield or a Plus freeze covers missed days automatically. Missed just yesterday? A short make-up session today earns it back for free (once a week).

Friends, duels or the feed don't load

These features use iCloud. Make sure you're signed into iCloud in the Settings app and connected to the internet.

How do I report someone?

Tap ··· on any post, profile, crew or duel and choose Report or Block. We review every report within 24 hours.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete all my data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
