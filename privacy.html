Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

150 Abs ("the app", "we") is made by Niccolo Valenti. We collect as little as possible, never sell data, and never track you across other apps or websites.

On your iPhone

Your profile (nickname, level, goals), workout history, streaks, points, reminder settings and progress photos are stored on your device.

Your private iCloud backup

If you're signed into iCloud, the app backs up your progress to your own private iCloud database so a reinstall or a new iPhone can restore it. Progress photos are included unless you turn this off in Settings → Backup. Only you can access this data; we cannot see it.

Community features (public iCloud)

When you use the friends ranking, duels, crews or the community feed, the following is stored in the app's public iCloud database and is visible to other 150 Abs users:

  • Your nickname, friend code, level, points, streak, total reps and the days you trained.
  • Check-ins you choose to post (day, level, reps, duration, optional caption and optional photo). Photos are resized and stripped of location and camera data before upload.
  • Your reactions, duel and crew memberships, and the name of crews you create.
  • Reports you send (used only for moderation).

Apple provides an anonymous, app-specific identifier for your iCloud account. We never see your Apple ID, name or email.

Anonymous usage analytics

We use PostHog to understand how the app is used (for example: onboarding completed, workout finished, a screen shown, a purchase made). Events use a random install identifier and never include your nickname, captions, photos or Health data. There is no session recording. You can turn this off in Settings → Share anonymous usage data.

Purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details; Apple tells the app only which purchases are active.

Invite codes (referral program)

If you join with someone's invite code, the code is saved with your profile. When you then make a purchase, the app stores a record in the public iCloud database so the person who invited you can see their earnings: their code, the product, price, currency, date, and Apple's signed transaction (used only to verify payouts). It contains no name, email, Apple ID or payment details. The app shows the person who invited you only totals, never who bought; the records themselves are stored in the public iCloud database. These records are deleted with the rest of your data when you use Delete all my data.

Apple Health

If you turn it on, finished sessions are written to Apple Health as workouts. We never read your Health data, and Health data is never sent to us or used for advertising.

Advertising

The app does not show ads and does not use the advertising identifier. If we ever add optional reward videos, this policy will be updated first and they will only play when you choose to watch one.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Delete all my data. This deletes your data on the device, your iCloud backup, and everything you created in the community (posts, reactions, reports, duels, crew memberships and your public profile).

Children

150 Abs is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
