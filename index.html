FAQ

Good questions.

Is 150 Abs free?

Yes. The full 30-day program, streaks, the weekly league, duels, crews and the community feed are free. 150 Abs Plus is optional and adds focus programs, streak freezes, every leaderboard, longer charts, before/after compare, unlimited duels and creating crews.

I've never trained abs. Can I do this?

Yes. Start at Beginner: sets are short and grow a little every week, and every exercise has a “How to” with an easier variation. If a day feels too hard, say so after the session and tomorrow gets lighter. 150 Abs is general fitness content, not medical advice, so check with a doctor first if you're pregnant, postpartum, injured or have a medical condition, and stop any exercise that hurts.

Do I need equipment or an account?

No equipment, just a bit of floor. There's no email or password either: the app works offline, and friends, duels and crews use your iCloud account behind the scenes.

What happens if I miss a day?

Missed just yesterday? A short make-up session today earns it back for free, once a week. A Streak Shield or a Plus freeze covers missed days automatically.

How do duels and crews work?

A duel is you and one friend: each of you does your own level's workout, and when you both finish the same day, your duo streak grows. A crew is up to 6 people sharing one streak and a weekly reps board. Invite with a link or a 6-character code.

How does Invite & earn work?

Friends who join with your code get 20% off Plus. When they pay for a purchase in the app, you earn 25% of what they paid, renewals included. Payouts are made on request once your balance reaches US$10. The full rules are in the referral program terms.

I got a new iPhone. Is my progress gone?

No. Sign into the same iCloud account, install 150 Abs and tap Restore my progress on the welcome screen. Your streak, history, friends, crews and duels come back.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → 150 Abs Plus, or in the app under Settings → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal (or before your free trial ends) to avoid being charged.

What do you do with my data?

As little as possible. The app shows no ads and never tracks you across other apps or websites, and this website has no analytics, trackers or cookies. Progress photos stay private, and Health data is never sent to us. You can delete everything from Settings → Delete all my data. Read the Privacy Policy.
